DAY 2 · BUILD 🧱

Build usable sentences

Notice the sentence pattern, then substitute information from your own life.

神様の愛を知りました。
かみさま の あい を しりました。
Kamisama no ai o shirimashita.
I came to know God’s love.
少しずつ、私の人生は変わりました。
すこしずつ、わたし の じんせい は かわりました。
Sukoshi zutsu, watashi no jinsei wa kawarimashita.
Little by little, my life changed.
Your turn: Say one true sentence about yourself. Then say it again without rōmaji.

DAY 3 · SPEAK 🗣️

Shadow → Retrieve → Speak

神様は私の支えです。神様は私の希望です。
かみさま は わたし の ささえ です。かみさま は わたし の きぼう です。
Kamisama wa watashi no sasae desu. Kamisama wa watashi no kibō desu.
God is my support. God is my hope.

Shadowing cycle: Natural ×2 → Slow ×1 → speak with text → hide rōmaji → speak again.

Pronunciation focus

Keep Japanese rhythm even. Do not stress words as strongly as in English. Listen for long vowels such as ō in rōmaji when they occur.

DAY 4 · CONNECT 🤝

🇯🇵 READ THE ROOM

The capstone is a personal story, not a canned evangelistic script. Keep it truthful, simple, and responsive to the listener.

Interactive judgment

A person gives you a brief or vulnerable answer. What should come first?
Prefer B. Mission language serves relationship, discernment, and truthful witness; it is not a script that overrides the person.
DAY 5 · MISSION LAB 🧭

Use the language without a fixed script

Mission simulation: imagine meeting someone in a café, church foyer, neighborhood, or community setting. Use at least three expressions from this week.

私はクリスチャンです。
わたし は くりすちゃん です。
Watashi wa kurisuchan desu.
I am a Christian.
人生には大変な時もありました。
じんせい には たいへん な とき も ありました。
Jinsei ni wa taihen na toki mo arimashita.
There have also been difficult times in my life.
神様の愛を知りました。
かみさま の あい を しりました。
Kamisama no ai o shirimashita.
I came to know God’s love.
60–90 second challenge: Start naturally. Ask or answer one question. Listen. Respond. Do not force the conversation toward faith if the context does not invite it.

Field reflection

What did I understand? Where did I get stuck? What cultural cue did I notice? What should I learn before the next encounter?

🎓 MODULE A FIELD ASSESSMENT

The Five-Minute Encounter

0:00–1:00
Greet and introduce yourself.
1:00–2:00
Discover something about the person.
2:00–3:00
Listen and respond.
3:00–4:00
Respond naturally to a possible spiritual opening.
4:00–5:00
Close respectfully.
